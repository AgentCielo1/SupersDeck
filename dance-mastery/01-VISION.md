# 01 — The Vision: A Life Lived Dancing

> "Living one's passions, despite the current hardships of life. Learning to become truly
> disciplined to follow the soul's desires and grow — not only spiritually, but physically as well."

## What we are building

A single system — part training program, part app, part daily companion — that takes one
person from *"lazy, unaccomplished, two weeks of motivation at a time"* to a disciplined,
daily-practicing dancer with real mastery, starting with **breaking (bboying)** and expanding
to **every dance the soul wants**: salsa, hip hop, ballet, tango, African dances, house,
popping, and beyond.

It is designed around one brutal, honest constraint:

**The user is an adult with a 9–5, limited free time, limited energy, and a track record of
quitting after week two.** Every design decision in this package is judged against that
person on their worst day — not their best.

## The three products in one

1. **The Curriculum** — the most complete, prerequisite-aware syllabus ever assembled for
   breaking (and a repeatable template for every other style), synthesized from the biggest
   schools, the smallest teachers, formal grading syllabi, and thousands of hours of online
   instruction. Foundation → Expert, nothing skipped, nothing vague. (Docs 02–04)

2. **The Motivation Engine** — a behavior-design system built from habit science and the
   retention mechanics of the stickiest products on earth, tuned specifically for physical
   practice after a workday. Its job: make quitting harder than continuing, and make
   *wanting to dance* the default state. (Doc 05)

3. **The Platform** — an app that teaches with high-quality **3D-animated instructors**
   (orbit the camera, slow it down, see the skeleton, loop the hard part), interactive
   lessons, camera-based form feedback where technology honestly allows it, and a
   curriculum modeled as data so progress, review, and daily session generation are
   automatic. Animation keeps us on the right side of copyright: we teach the *knowledge*
   (moves, methods, progressions — which are not copyrightable), expressed through our own
   original animated performances. (Doc 06)

## Design principles (the constitution)

Every feature, lesson, and mechanic must obey these. When in doubt, return here.

1. **The session must survive the worst day.** There is always a version of today's
   practice that takes 10 minutes, in street clothes, in a small room. A completed tiny
   session counts fully. Zero days are the only failure.
2. **Foundation is sacred, but joy comes first.** The curriculum is foundation-first
   (the breaking community is unanimous on this), but every single session ends with
   free dancing to music you love. You leave the floor wanting more, never depleted.
3. **Identity over outcomes.** The system's language and mechanics reinforce *"I am a
   dancer who trains daily"* — not "I am trying to get abs" or "I must unlock windmills
   by March." Moves arrive as consequences of identity, not deadlines.
4. **Never miss twice.** Missing a day is planned for, not punished. Streaks bend, they
   don't break; repair mechanics exist by design because relapse is part of the model.
5. **The body is prepared before the move is attempted.** Strength, mobility, and joint
   conditioning are woven into the skill tree as real prerequisites. Injury is the #1
   silent killer of self-taught breakers; we treat prep like gymnastics coaches do.
6. **Every move is a node in a graph, not a video in a list.** Prerequisites, drills,
   common mistakes, form checkpoints, and review schedules are data. The app always knows
   what you should touch today.
7. **Show, don't describe.** 3D animation with free camera, tempo control, skeleton
   overlay, and loop-the-hard-part beats any talking-head video. Both views (follow-along
   mirror view + orbit inspection view) for every move.
8. **Respect the cultures.** Breaking, African dances, salsa, tango — each has history,
   music, and community. Lessons carry the context; styles are credited to their sources
   and teachers. This is a bridge to the cultures, not a strip-mining of them.
9. **Music is the drug.** Musicality is trained from day one, and the system leans on
   music as the primary motivator — sessions are built on playlists that make *not*
   moving harder than moving.
10. **Solo-first, but never alone.** Everything works alone in a bedroom. But the system
    creates artificial "performance gravity": recorded progress clips, virtual battles,
    community checkpoints — because every study and every dance culture says the social
    loop is what keeps people dancing for decades.

## Who this is for (v1: a single user — you)

- Self-taught history: has learned moves from YouTube before, understands follow-along
  learning, knows the pain of unstructured progress.
- Physical base: previously built move-specific strength through guided exercise; currently
  detrained. Assume a returning beginner body with a fast re-learning curve.
- Time budget: realistically 20–45 minutes on weekdays, more on weekends — but the system
  must survive on 10.
- Psychological pattern: strong starts, week-two collapse, guilt loop, restart months later.
  The Motivation Engine (Doc 05) is aimed directly at this loop.
- Long-term desire: not one dance — *dance itself*. Breaking is the entry point because it
  is the current passion and the hardest physical build (its strength base transfers
  everywhere).

## What "Master Bboy" means here (the summit we're climbing)

Mastery is defined by the same axes the sport's own judges use (the WDSF/Olympic Trivium
system) plus the culture's own standards:

- **Technique** — clean execution across all five pillars: toprock, footwork, freezes,
  power, transitions.
- **Vocabulary/Variety** — breadth across the whole skill tree, not ten polished tricks.
- **Musicality** — hitting the music, riding breaks, dancing *to* the song, not near it.
- **Originality/Character** — your own flavor, signature moves, style that is recognizably
  yours.
- **Execution under pressure** — full rounds, battle-ready, no crashes.
- **Body mastery** — the strength, flexibility, and conditioning of an elite mover;
  training every day and recovering well.

## The bigger arc

Breaking is Mountain One. The platform is built so that every later style — salsa, ballet,
tango, Afro styles — is "just" a new curriculum pack on the same engine: same skill-graph
model, same motivation engine, same animated-instructor pipeline, same daily session
generator. Doc 04 defines that template and seeds it with the first five styles.

The end state: a person who trains their body every single day because they want to, who
can walk into a cypher, a salsa social, a tango milonga, or a West African dance circle
and *belong* — and a platform that can take anyone else on the same journey.
